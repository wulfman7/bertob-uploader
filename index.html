Bertob Uploader

Bertob Uploader is a private tool used by Wulf Willis to publish his own videos to his own YouTube channels: Bertob Generations, Bertob Timers, Bertob Sounds and related channels.

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, audience settings and publishing schedule. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
