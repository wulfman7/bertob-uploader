Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Bertob Uploader is a personal tool operated by Wulf Willis for publishing videos to his own YouTube channels. It is not available to the public.

What data the app uses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app receives an OAuth token with permission to upload videos and manage video details (title, description, thumbnail, visibility and schedule) on that owner's YouTube channels. It reads only the channel and video information needed to do this.

How data is stored

The OAuth token is stored as an encrypted secret in a private GitHub repository controlled by the channel owner. Video files and their details are supplied by the owner. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account security page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from making any further changes.
